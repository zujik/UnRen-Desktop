renpy.full_restart

Function: renpy.full_restart( )

This causes a full restart of Ren'Py. This clears the store and configuration, and re-runs init blocks before branching off to the start label. It's very close to what happens when we quit out and re-run the interpreter, except for some caches not being cleared.

Example

'The author would like to thank everyone who makes original
 English-language bishoujo games, and the people on the Lemmasoft forums
 who encouraged him.'

"We can't wait to see what you do with this. Good luck!"

"It took you %(minutes)d minutes and %(seconds)d seconds to
 finish this demo."
